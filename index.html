Jesus’ disciples continued sharing the Gospel through adversity. The Disciples’ Walk is a community challenge to reflect on their faith, hear others’ perspectives, and learn to speak about our own beliefs with courage, gentleness, and respect. 01 Approach Start a respectful conversation. Ask permission to talk, be considerate of others’ time, and accept a “no” graciously. 02 Understand Ask what gave the disciples courage. Listen openly to people’s experiences, beliefs, and questions. 03 Share When invited, share what Jesus and the Gospel mean to you. Lead with compassion, never pressure. OUR INSPIRATION “Always be prepared to give an answer … with gentleness and respect.” 1 PETER 3:15 · PARAPHRASED EXCERPT BE PART OF THE JOURNEY Every walk begins with a step.
