Why we walk

To understand the heart behind the mission.

Jesus’ disciples continued sharing the Gospel through adversity. The Disciples’ Walk is a community challenge to reflect on their faith, hear others’ perspectives, and learn to speak about our own beliefs with courage, gentleness, and respect.

01

Approach

Start a respectful conversation. Ask permission to talk, be considerate of others’ time, and accept a “no” graciously.

02

Understand

Ask what gave the disciples courage. Listen openly to people’s experiences, beliefs, and questions.

03

Share

When invited, share what Jesus and the Gospel mean to you. Lead with compassion, never pressure.

Our inspiration
“Always be prepared to give an answer … with gentleness and respect.”
1 PETER 3:15 · PARAPHRASED EXCERPT
Be part of the journey

Every walk begins with a step.

Join a community learning from the disciples’ faith and courage. You can participate individually or with a group, share reflections, and help us understand the impact of genuine conversations.

Free to join · Everyone welcome

For your safety, participate with a trusted group, have conversations in public places, respect personal boundaries, and avoid collecting strangers’ personal details without their consent.
