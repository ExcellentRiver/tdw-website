Why we walk

To understand the heart behind the mission.

Jesus’ disciples continued sharing the Gospel through adversity. The Disciples’ Walk is a community challenge to reflect on their faith, hear others’ perspectives, and learn to speak about our own beliefs with courage, gentleness, and respect.

01

Approach

Start a respectful conversation. Ask permission to talk, be considerate of others’ time, and accept a “no” graciously.

02

Understand

Ask what gave the disciples courage. Listen openly to people’s experiences, beliefs, and questions.

03

Share

When invited, share what Jesus and the Gospel mean to you. Lead with compassion, never pressure.
