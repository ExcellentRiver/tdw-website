Why we walk

Understanding the heart behind the mission.

The Disciples' Walk is a community challenge inspired by the courage and faith of Jesus' disciples. We seek to understand others, build genuine connections, and share the Gospel with gentleness and respect.

01

Approach

Begin a respectful conversation. Be considerate of people's time and always respect their choice not to talk.

02

Understand

Listen with genuine curiosity. Seek to understand people's experiences, perspectives, and questions.

03

Share

When welcomed, share your faith and what the Gospel means to you with compassion and respect.

Our inspiration
"Always be prepared to give an answer … with gentleness and respect."

1 PETER 3:15 · PARAPHRASED EXCERPT
